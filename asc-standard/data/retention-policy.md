# Data Retention Policy

Safety evidence and replay logs are retained per program compliance requirements.

## TASC EU North-Star Baseline

- Signed operational log minimum retention: 6 months.
- EvidenceMap and conformance artifact retention: program-defined, not less than the incident investigation window.
- Incident response windows: 15 days (standard), 2 days (widespread infringement), 10 days (fatal event case handling).
