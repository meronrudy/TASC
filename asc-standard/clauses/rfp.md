# TASC RFP Clause Pack v0.3

## SECTION [X]: TRUSTED AVIONICS SAFETY CONTRACT (TASC) ARTIFACTS (MANDATORY)

### 1. Definitions

- **TASC Assurance Pack**: Versioned artifact bundle defined by `schemas/assurance-pack.schema.json`.
- **TASC Conformance CLI**: `tasc-verify` at version pinned in contract.
- **EvidenceMap**: Machine-readable topology + artifact hash index as defined in `schemas/evidence-map.schema.json`.

### 2. Required Deliverables

Offeror SHALL provide for each safety-relevant release:

1. EvidenceMap `v0.1` JSON.
2. TASC conformance report generated by pinned `tasc-verify` version.
3. Signed operational log + verification instructions.
4. Deterministic replay recipe + replay SLA statement.
5. Trust-anchor attestation evidence (minimum TA2).
6. Incident templates and retention policy declaration.
7. Rekor and mirror transparency proofs.
8. Shipment Eligibility Certificate (signed procurement object).
9. Underwriter Confidence Packet (signed procurement object).
10. Procurement Bid Packet (signed procurement object).
11. Recycler Intake Passport (signed procurement object) when lifecycle stage is `decommission` or `recycle`.

Each signed procurement object SHALL include:
- `policyPackId` and `policyPackVersion`
- `inputHash` and `bundleDigest`
- signer identity and `trustAnchorLevel`
- validity interval (`notBeforeUtc`, `notAfterUtc`)
- verifier instructions (`command`, `requiredChecks`)
- signature envelope and standalone artifact reference (`artifactRef.path`, `artifactRef.sha256`)

### 3. Verification and Acceptance Testing

Buyer SHALL execute:

```bash
cargo run --manifest-path tools/tasc-verify/Cargo.toml -- verify \
  --bundle <delivered_assurance_pack.json> \
  --profile <profile> \
  --policy eu-north-star \
  --require-ta TA2 \
  --require-transparency rekor,mirror
```

Bid is NON-RESPONSIVE if verification fails.

### 4. Service Levels

- Replay package available within `[X]` hours of request.
- Initial incident pack within `[Y]` hours; full pack within `[Z]` days.
- Signed-log export available for full retention window.

### 5. Retention and Access

Vendor SHALL retain signed logs and EvidenceMap artifacts for at least six months (or stricter contractual window) and provide audit access to Buyer.

### 6. Remedies

Failure to deliver conforming artifacts or failed verification constitutes material breach and requires corrective action plan within `[N]` days.
